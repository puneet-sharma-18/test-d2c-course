# D2C Insider AI Bootcamp, Student Handbook

Over this weekend you hire, onboard and put to work ten AI teammates for your brand. Not modules. Not exercises. Teammates with a job description, an inbox, a tool kit and a shipping bar.

By Sunday evening every one of them is running on your brand, your data and your competitors, and you have a 90 day plan for what they do next.

This handbook is the map. Each session has its own file. Open one at a time. Every prompt you need is inside the session file.

---

## How this runs

Everything happens in the **Claude Code desktop app**, the Mac or Windows application. You will not open a terminal. You install an app, point it at a folder, and type in a chat box.

Three things you will do over and over, so learn them once in Session 0:

1. **Type `/`** to see every teammate available and pick one.
2. **Watch the Tasks pane** when a subagent runs, so you can see work happening rather than waiting at a blank screen.
3. **Click a file path** in the chat to read what your teammate wrote. You never need Finder or Explorer.

## The two day arc

### Day 1, Intelligence and Content

Four primitives, in order. Each unlocks a teammate.

| Primitive | What it is | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **`CLAUDE.md`** | A persistent profile of your brand, voice, products and customers | Every later teammate reads it before doing anything |
| **Skills** | Reusable playbooks Claude loads when they are relevant | What turns a chat tool into teammates |
| **Connectors and MCP** | The plug between Claude and Shopify, Drive, Gmail | Lets teammates read live data, not just files |
| **Subagents** | Teammates that run in their own context for big jobs | Multi piece deliverables without filling up your session |

If by Saturday evening you can answer *what is in CLAUDE.md, what is a skill, what is a connector, what is a subagent*, Day 2 is just doing.

### Day 2, Growth and Operations

| Primitive | What it is | What you ship |
|---|---|---|
| **Parallel dispatch** | One parent subagent fans out to several children | Ad variants across multiple angles at once |
| **Live data iteration** | Read real numbers, diagnose, rewrite, save as draft | Rewritten PDPs and marketplace listings |
| **Triggers** | When X happens, do Y | SOPs and retention messages tied to real events |
| **Routines** | Scheduled runs inside the desktop app | A brief that lands every Monday without you asking |
| **The Captain pattern** | A subagent that reads across every other teammate | The 90 day roadmap and one cross cutting call |

## Before you arrive

**Fill `templates/brand-brain/`.** Copy that folder to the repo root, rename it `brand-brain`, and put in whatever you have: reviews, competitor notes, product data, your unit economics, and above all samples of your own writing.

Founders who arrive with that folder filled walk out with reports built on real data. Founders who arrive cold spend Saturday morning catching up, and every downstream session inherits the gap.

**Fill what you have and leave the rest.** Session 1 interviews you for anything missing. A gap is a question you answer out loud, not a blocker.

The single highest value folder is `brand-brain/voice-dna/`. Five things you actually wrote. Not written for this, written by you, already sent. That folder is the difference between output that sounds like your brand and output that sounds like every other brand in your category.

## When you cannot picture the output

Two brands are worked all the way through in [`examples/`](examples/README.md): **Little Lab**, baby skincare under CDSCO, sold mostly on marketplaces, and **The Paan Legacy**, gourmet paan under FSSAI, sold on its own site and quick commerce with a franchise and corporate side.

Open whichever is closer to your category. Every session file links to the right folder inside them.

Two things to read in either one. First, `brand-brain/` before `my-work/`, because what a teammate was given is more instructive than what it wrote. Second, the flags. Both examples are full of gaps, refusals and missing licence numbers, left in on purpose. That is what real output looks like.

## Two scopes, every session

Every skill and subagent asks you to pick scope before it starts.

**Default** is sized for a Pro plan and the length of a session. It produces less and teaches the same thing.

**Power** is for a Max plan or for running again at home. More angles, more pieces, more segments.

Pick Default in the room. Re-run in Power on Sunday night when nobody is waiting for you.

---

## After the bootcamp

Re-run any teammate any week by opening their session file and repeating the steps. They compound. Brand Brain gets richer, Market Analyst keeps tracking, Growth Analyst sharpens as your data grows.

When you want to add more tools, read [`resources.md`](resources.md) before installing anything you saw on a podcast. It lists what is worth adding, in order, and what to skip.

---

## Stuck

Every session file ends with a troubleshooting section for that session specifically. Beyond that, [`references/module-0-setup/troubleshooting.md`](references/module-0-setup/troubleshooting.md) covers install and repo problems.

In the room, raise a hand. A TA pairs in within a minute. The cohort goal is everyone shipping by Sunday evening, not everyone finishing alone.
