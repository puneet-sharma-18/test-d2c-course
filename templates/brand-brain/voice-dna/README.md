# voice-dna/

**The most valuable folder in this repo.**

Things you actually wrote. Not things about your brand, things by your brand.

Every teammate reads these to learn your sentence rhythm, your vocabulary and your tells. Five real samples change the output more than any amount of instruction.

## What to put here

One file per sample. Name them so the format is obvious.

- `pdp-{product}.md` — a product page you wrote
- `email-welcome.md` — a real email that went out
- `instagram-{topic}-caption.md` — a caption you posted
- `whatsapp-replies.md` — real replies to customers, lightly redacted
- `packaging-thankyou-insert.md` — the card in the box
- `founder-note-on-{topic}.md` — anything you wrote in first person

## The rule

**Real or nothing.** Do not write a sample for this folder. A polished thing you produced for this exercise teaches your teammates a voice you do not actually use, and they will use it in everything they write afterwards.

A messy WhatsApp reply you sent at 11pm is worth more here than a paragraph you drafted this morning.

## A worked version

`examples/the-paan-legacy/brand-brain/voice-dna/` holds nine real samples for a working brand. Read two of them to calibrate.
