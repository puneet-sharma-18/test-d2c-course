# brand-brain, your input folder

Copy this whole folder to the repo root and rename it `brand-brain`. On Mac, drag it up a level in Finder and rename. On Windows, copy and paste it into the project folder and rename.

```
templates/brand-brain/   ->   brand-brain/
```

Everything you put in here is read by your teammates. Nothing in here is committed to git, so your real business data stays on your machine.

**Fill what you have. Leave the rest.** A file with three honest lines beats a file with three hundred invented ones. Session 1 interviews you for whatever is missing, so a gap here is not a blocker, it is just a question you answer out loud later.

## What each file is for

| File | Feeds | What good looks like |
|---|---|---|
| `positioning.md` | Session 1, Section 3 | One paragraph. Why the brand exists, who it is for, what you will never do. |
| `products.csv` | Session 1, Section 4 | One row per SKU. Price, margin, units sold, conversion rate, rating. |
| `competitors.md` | Session 1 and 2 | Three to five brands, one line each on why they matter. |
| `reviews.md` | Session 1 and 2 | Real customer reviews, pasted. Fifty or more makes Session 2 sharp. |
| `unit-economics.md` | Session 1 and 9 | CAC, LTV, AOV, margin, channel mix. Your numbers, however rough. |
| `nominated-skus.md` | Sessions 3 and 7 | The five SKUs and one page you want the work aimed at. |
| `ads/ad-copy-bank.md` | Session 6 | Recent ad copy that ran, with any performance note you have. |
| `voice-dna/` | Sessions 1, 2 and 6 | Things you actually wrote. PDPs, emails, captions, WhatsApp replies. |

## The one that matters most

`voice-dna/`. Everything else can be reconstructed from data. Your voice cannot. Five real samples of your own writing is the difference between output that sounds like you and output that sounds like every other brand in your category.

Paste in whatever exists. A WhatsApp reply you sent a customer counts. A packaging insert counts. It does not need to be polished, it needs to be yours.

## A worked version

`examples/the-paan-legacy/brand-brain/` is this same folder, filled in for a real gourmet paan brand. Open it when you are unsure how much detail a file wants.
