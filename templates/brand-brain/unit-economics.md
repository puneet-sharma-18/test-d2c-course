# Unit Economics, {Your Brand}

> Your numbers, however rough. Where you are guessing, say so in the line. The Growth Analyst in Session 9 would rather have a hedged number it can label than a confident one it cannot.

## Headline
Revenue per month, growth rate, gross margin, and the one number you are least comfortable with.

## AOV
Blended, and by channel if you have it.

## Gross margin
Blended, and per SKU if you have it. Say whether it is COGS only or fully loaded.

## CAC
Target, current blended, and per platform. Say which attribution you used.

## LTV
Your estimate and how you got there. If you have no real cohort, say that.

## Channel mix
Revenue share by channel over the last 7, 28 or 90 days. Include offline, franchise and corporate if they exist, even when there is no clean system of record for them.

## Repeat rate
Whatever you track, and on what basis, order count or customer count.

## Where I am hedging
The list that makes this file trustworthy. Every number you would not defend in a meeting, and why.
