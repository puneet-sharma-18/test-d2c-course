# Customer Reviews, {Your Brand}

> Paste real reviews. Amazon, Flipkart, Shopify, Zomato, Swiggy, Google, WhatsApp, anywhere. Keep the date, the platform, the SKU and the star rating where you have them, because Session 2 cuts sentiment by all four.

**Include the bad ones.** A set with no one and two star reviews tells you why people stay and nothing about why anyone left, and the second question is the expensive one.

Fifty or more makes Session 2 sharp. Under thirty and it reports signals rather than patterns, honestly and out loud.
