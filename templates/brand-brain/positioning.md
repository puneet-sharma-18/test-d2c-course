# Positioning, {Your Brand}

> Replace everything below. One paragraph is enough. Write it the way you would explain the brand to a friend, not the way you would pitch it to an investor.

Cover these, in any order:

- **Why this brand exists.** The actual story, not the deck version.
- **Who it is for.** Age, city, income if you know it, and what they were doing before you existed.
- **The wedge.** What you do that the category does not.
- **How customers behave.** Do they buy monthly, seasonally, once?
- **Who you compete with**, and on what.
- **What you will never do.** Two or three anti-positioning lines. "Never compete on price." "Never use stock imagery." "Never use the word premium."
- **Compliance.** FSSAI, CDSCO, Ayush, anything that governs what you are allowed to claim.

The last two matter most. Every teammate reads them before writing a word.
