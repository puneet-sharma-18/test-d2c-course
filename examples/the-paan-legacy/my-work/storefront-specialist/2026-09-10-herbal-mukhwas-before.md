## Gap analysis, Herbal Mukhwas

The page leads with: unknown, but the product name and the `hand-roasted` tag suggest craft and process, which is what both reviewers praised.
Customers actually care about: **grams for the money. 2 of the 2 mukhwas reviews in the Voice of Customer report raise pack size**, and both praise the roasting in the same sentence. "Pack size small for the price" and "Wish the pack was 300g instead of 200g for the price".
Competitors emphasise, and we do not: **quantity and a price anchor.** The Market Analyst report found Royal Mouth Freshners running a 5 x 100g combo gift pack and Shahi Spoon running an explicit "Mouth Fresheners Under ₹300" creative, both live in the last week. Unanswered here, but asked constantly in support: **there is no support-ticket data.** The Voice of Customer report contains zero tickets. The nearest signal is a WhatsApp allergen thread, where the honest answer was that this SKU is gluten free and Granola Goodness is not.
Biggest conversion blocker, best guess: **the page is arguing craft while the shopper is doing arithmetic.** Every other page in this range says the price gap out loud and explains it. This one appears not to, and it is the only SKU where the customer can count the difference.
