## Pages picked, and why

The rule is the two lowest-converting among the top five by orders. By units over 90 days the top five are Meetha Paan, Almond Delight, Herbal Mukhwas, Granola Goodness and Dry Fruit Meetha. Of those, **Herbal Mukhwas converts at 2.0% and Granola Goodness at 2.1%**, the two lowest. Those are the pages.

**One redirect worth considering.** Masala Cranberry converts at **1.9%, the lowest in the catalogue**, and sits one rank outside the window because it is sixth by volume. Both prior reports point at it: the Voice of Customer report disagrees with the "retire it" framing in `CLAUDE.md` and calls it a page problem, and the Content Lead skipped it for social on the grounds that it belongs in product-page work. Say the word and it takes twenty minutes.

## Top five reads

1. **The rewrite most likely to lift conversion: Granola Goodness.** It changes what the page is for. Today it sells a curiosity product. The rewrite sells it as the front door, says in the first line what the product actually is, since a granola page on a paan site is genuinely confusing, and downsells to the ₹179 Meetha Paan in the body. Two documented crossovers in the Voice of Customer report, both in the same direction and none in reverse, justify treating entry as the job rather than basket value.

2. **Fix this week, high impact and one day of work: put the spit answer above the fold.** Five of twenty-seven customer messages turn on it, one customer asked before paying, and another said the page claim is why they bought. The line already exists as the brand refrain. This is moving text, not writing it.

3. **The rewrite needing a voice check: Herbal Mukhwas.** It applies the brand's own price-gap device, name the cheaper alternative out loud then explain the gap, to mukhwas for the first time. It works on the paan pages. On this page it has to survive the fact that the customer can actually count the difference in grams, which they cannot do with a hand-rolled paan. Read the "Why 200g and not 400g" section and decide whether it reads as confident or defensive.

4. **The observation worth investigating properly: the free insulated box is hidden in a free-text order note.** The upgrade is already free and already built. Customers who do not know the magic phrase keep getting melted product, and that failure is priced at 3 to 5 margin points on two SKUs plus a CAC drift already traced to hot pin codes. This is not a copy problem, it is a cart problem.

5. **Flagged, and blocking: the Herbal Mukhwas price argument rests on two placeholders.** `{competitor-price}` and `{multiple}` carry the entire "Why 200g" section, and every competitor price in the Market Analyst report is marked unverified because the marketplace pages are proxy-blocked. Check one real 400g pack before filling them. Do not write those numbers from memory.
