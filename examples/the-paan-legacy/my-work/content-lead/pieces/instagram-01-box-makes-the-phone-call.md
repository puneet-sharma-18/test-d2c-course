## Why it works

The strongest finding in the Voice of Customer report is that the gift box recruits its own customers, documented four separate times, and it runs on a phone call nobody can see. This caption says the loop out loud to the person who starts it. It does not ask them to gift. It tells them what happens when they do, in their own words.

## SAFETY FLAGS

1. **Fulfilment promise that may not exist.** The CTA says "I will write the card by hand". `CLAUDE.md` Section 1 says the founder signs the packaging insert by hand, which is not the same thing, and the Voice of Customer report recommends a card in the box as something to *build*, not something that exists: "There is nothing in the box that makes that step easy. A card with a name and a QR code would convert a phone call into a tracked first order." Either build the card first or change the CTA to "Reply and tell me who it is going to." Do not promise a hand-written card at volume unless you can do it every time.
2. **Passed.** Banned words, none. Regulated claims, none beyond the safe factual list, tobacco free, no supari, no zarda, hand rolled. Numbers all traceable: "four times" to Voice of Customer Theme 4, prices and format to `CLAUDE.md` Section 4, "two paans per person" to the Section 7 voice rules. Quote verbatim from Theme 4, attributed as the report attributes it. No competitor named. No price competing.
