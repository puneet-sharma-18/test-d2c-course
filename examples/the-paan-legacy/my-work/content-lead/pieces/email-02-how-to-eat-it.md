# Email, lifecycle, post-purchase
Scheduled: 2026-09-17, Thursday. Calendar day 8.
Trigger: sent on the day the box is marked delivered.
Topic: How to eat it, and how many people it is actually for.
Theme it serves: Eaten at a table, never alone. VoC Theme 3, 8 of 27, the largest theme in the set.
Source: `my-work/voice-of-customer/2026-09-09-voc-report.md`, Theme 3, Theme 5 and the persona card "The Sunday table restorer". Format, storage and allergen facts from `CLAUDE.md` Section 4 and the FSSAI claim wall in Section 7.
Audience: every first-time buyer, on delivery day.

## Subject line

Your box has landed. Read this before you open it.

## Preview text

Two paans per person. Take one out and eat it in one piece.

## Body

The box is with you. Six paan, wrapped one by one.

Do three things before anything else.

Put it in the fridge. Not the freezer. Straight away.

Take one out. Eat it in one piece. Do not bite it and put it down. It is built to go in whole and that is the difference between this and the one at the shop.

Eat, do not spit. That is the whole point of it.

Now the part people get wrong.

Two paans per person is the right number. Six in a box is three people. Not one person for six days. Eight of our customers wrote to us and every one of them named somebody else in the house. A husband. A mother. A mother in law. The kids.

> "Meetha Paan is the new evening ritual at our place. Tobacco free is the reason my mother in law eats it with me."
> review, marketplace

That is what this is for. Traditionally taken after meals, at a table with other people at it.

Best within 14 days of dispatch. It is not going to sit in your fridge for a month and be the same thing, and I would rather tell you that than let you find out.

Two more things I owe you.

Almond Delight contains tree nuts, almond, and cardamom, rose and fennel. Meetha Paan has cardamom, rose and fennel. If somebody at your table has an allergy, check the box before you hand it round.

If it arrived soft, or the edges had gone, write back and tell me. Every complaint I have ever had is about how it travelled, never about how it tasted. That is a known weak spot and we have been slow to solve it.

I read this inbox myself.

Puneet.

## Why it works

Theme 3 is the largest theme in the report and it is the one that most changes behaviour after purchase, not before it. Repeat rate is 30%, the strongest number in the business, so the delivery-day email is the highest-value lifecycle slot the brand has. This piece instructs rather than sells, tells the customer to eat less rather than more, and opens the door on the condition-on-arrival complaint before the customer has to raise it, which is the pattern in Theme 5 where three customers defended the product while reporting the problem.

## CTA

"Reply and tell me who else ate it. That is the only thing I want to know."

## SAFETY FLAGS

1. **Overstated claim, fix before send.** The line "Every complaint I have ever had is about how it travelled, never about how it tasted" overstates a caveated finding. The Voice of Customer report says "zero of 27 messages complain about flavour", and then says plainly that the set is positively skewed with no 1 or 2 star review in it, that 27 messages is a signal rather than a pattern, and that `products.csv` holds 420 reviews that have not been exported. `CLAUDE.md` also shows Masala Cranberry at 4.3. Change to "Every complaint in the last set of reviews I read was about how it travelled, never about how it tasted", or pull the full 420 review export and then say it properly.
2. **Passed.** Banned words, none. Health claim ceiling held, "traditionally taken after meals" verbatim and nothing further. Allergens per SKU exactly as the `CLAUDE.md` claim wall states them. Storage and shelf life verbatim. No upsell and no cross-sell, the CTA asks a question and sells nothing. Quote verbatim from Voice of Customer Theme 3. No competitor named.
