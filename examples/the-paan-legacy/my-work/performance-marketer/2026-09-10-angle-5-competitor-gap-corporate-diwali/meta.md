## SAFETY FLAGS

Seven checks run on all five ads. Nothing was silently fixed.

1. **BLOCKING. The ₹599 price may no longer be valid.** `CLAUDE.md` S7 says corporate pre-order pricing locks 31 August. That date passed 10 days ago. ₹599 appears in A5-1 in copy and in the description, and in the Google sets. Either the lock is extended and the new date is set, or the price comes out and becomes `{placeholder}`. The Content Lead flagged the same missing lock date as blocking on the corporate email. **This is one decision that unblocks two teammates' work.**
2. **"Kid-safe" is removed from the copy, deliberately, and it was the buyer's own word.** The VoC corporate persona lists three filters, tobacco-free, vegetarian and kid-safe. The first two are named safe factual claims in the `CLAUDE.md` S7 FSSAI table. The third is not a claim this brand can make, and it is the opposite of safe on Almond Delight, which declares tree nuts. Dropping the buyer's strongest word costs conversion, and writing it costs more. If the founder wants the idea, the honest version is the allergen declaration printed on the sleeve, not a claim in an ad.
3. **The corporate persona is n equals 1.** One message in 27. The VoC report labels it "treat as a lead, not a finding". Three of the five ads in this angle are built on it. That is a deliberate bet on the Market Analyst's independent evidence, 13 live competitor ads in the same motion, rather than on the customer sample. Worth knowing that is what it is.
4. **Corporate fulfilment outside Mumbai is unverified.** A5-5 names Bengaluru. The kitchen is in Mumbai, the product is best within 14 days of dispatch and asks to be refrigerated on receipt, and the worst logistics complaint on file is 5 days to Kolkata. An 80 box order to a Bengaluru office is an operational promise nobody in this chain has confirmed. Confirm before this ad runs, not after it converts.
5. **The 80 box order is not in any ad copy, on purpose.** It is real, it is in the VoC report, and it is n equals 1 and undated. As a number in an ad it reads as a scale claim the brand cannot repeat on demand. It stays in the brief as reasoning and out of the creative.
6. **"Batches ship from 10 October" is a dated operational promise.** From `CLAUDE.md` S7. If the kitchen's Diwali schedule has moved, this line moves with it. A missed dispatch date on a gifting ad is the expensive kind of wrong.
7. **Account status blocks the launch.** Meta account 1145209407174523 is DISABLED, flagged for unusual activity, per `my-work/growth-analyst/2026-09-10-brief.md`. Launch into 888048036093502. Note also that the account has been dark 22 days, so a relight re-enters the learning phase, and the season this creative has to carry opens 5 October. That is the real cost of the 22 days.
8. **No budget, ROAS or CPC target anywhere in this file.** The only live benchmark is 62 clicks from ₹1,615.56. `{placeholder}` until a week of data exists.
9. **No competitor is named and no comparison is made,** although this entire angle exists because of what competitors are doing. Every competitor price in the Market Analyst report is marked unverified with three storefronts blocked by the egress proxy, so no comparative claim would have been sourceable. The competitive insight sits in the targeting and the timing, never in the words.

Clean: no never-list word, no deprecated word, no health claim, every quote string-matched verbatim against the VoC report, no em dash, every character count verified.
