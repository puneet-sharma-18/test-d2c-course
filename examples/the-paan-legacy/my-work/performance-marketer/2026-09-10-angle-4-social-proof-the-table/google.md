## SAFETY FLAGS

1. **G4-A H1 is a replacement, not an addition.** The banned headline is live now. Adding H1 without removing "Premium Paan Online, Tobacco-Free." leaves the violation running. Fix at source, as `CLAUDE.md` S7 says. The site title tag carries two banned words as well, per the Market Analyst report, and that is a separate fix this file does not cover.
2. **Prices from a March 2026 sheet.** ₹179 and ₹299 appear in seven headlines and descriptions across the three sets. Re-check before these go live.
3. **"Refrigerate on receipt" and "Best within 14 days of dispatch" are storage instructions, not selling lines.** They are in `CLAUDE.md` S7 as shelf-life facts and they are here because a food ad that hides them buys a return. They cost characters and they are worth it.
4. **No health claim in any set.** "Traditionally taken after meals" is the ceiling and it is not used, because it does not fit 30 characters and because none of these sets needs it. Nothing about digestion, gut, immunity or detox appears.
5. **No competitor name and no comparative claim in any set.** Every competitor price in the Market Analyst report is marked unverified, with three storefronts blocked by the egress proxy, so a comparison would be unsourced.
6. **"Ask On WhatsApp" as a sitelink assumes a click-to-chat link exists.** `CLAUDE.md` S2 says the founder answers the WhatsApp inbox himself, so the channel exists. Whether it is wired to a Google sitelink is `{placeholder}`.
