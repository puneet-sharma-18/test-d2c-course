# Template, Message to customers with an order in flight
Use when: stock has run out after an order was placed but before it shipped.
Tone: first person, signed, no hedging. Sent on WhatsApp where possible.

## Body

> Hi {first-name}, this is Puneet.
> Your order of {sku} was placed on {order-date} and I do not have the stock to ship it on time. That is my planning error, not a supplier problem.
> Two options, whichever suits you:
> 1. I hold your order and ship on {new-date}, confirmed, not estimated.
> 2. I refund it in full today, no questions.
> If this was a gift for {occasion}, tell me the date it needs to arrive and I will tell you honestly whether I can make it.
> Reply 1 or 2.
> Puneet, The Paan Legacy

## Rules
- Name the real cause. Do not write "unprecedented demand".
- Offer the refund first-class, not as the grudging option.
- Ask about the gift deadline. A late gift is a lost referral and the referral is the acquisition channel.
- Never use this to upsell.

## What not to write
- "We are committed to ongoing improvement."
- "We apologise for any inconvenience caused."
- Anything that makes the customer chase a second reply to get their money.
