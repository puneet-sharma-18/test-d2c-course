# Template, Reply to a condition or quality complaint
Use when: a customer reports a product that arrived melted, soft, broken or otherwise not right.
Tone: admit it flat, refund without being asked twice, say what changes. First person, signed.

## Body

> Hi {first-name}, this is Puneet. I am sorry.
> {Specific-defect} on a {shipping-lane} shipment in {month} is a known weak spot for us and we have been slow to solve it. I am refunding the {sku} portion of your order today, it will show in {refund-window}.
> For your next order to {city} we will ship in the insulated box at no extra cost. You do not need to ask for it, I have put a note on your name.
> If you want to tell me what the box looked like when it arrived, send a photo. It helps me find whether this was the kitchen or the courier.
> Thanks for telling me instead of just not ordering again.
> Puneet, The Paan Legacy

## Rules
- Refund in the first three lines. Not after an explanation.
- Name the weakness plainly. "Known weak spot, slow to solve it" is the house register.
- Never ask the customer to return a food product before refunding.
- Never write a health or safety reassurance you cannot substantiate. If the customer asks whether the product was safe to eat, escalate to the founder, do not answer from a template.

## What not to write
- "As per our policy."
- Any claim about the product being unaffected in taste or quality unless the customer said so first.
