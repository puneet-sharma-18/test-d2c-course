## Description, 300 to 500 words

**This is paan you swallow.**

Most paan sold in India is made to be chewed and spat out, because it is built around tobacco and supari. This one is not. There is no tobacco in it, no supari and no zarda. You eat the whole thing, betel leaf included.

**What is in it**

Rose. Gulkand. Fennel. Cardamom. A thread of coconut. Wrapped in a betel leaf. That is the complete list and it is printed on the box, so you can check it against what you are eating. Vegetarian.

**How it is made**

Hand rolled in a small kitchen in Mumbai, in batches, on the morning it is dispatched. It is not made in advance and stored. Six paan to a box, wrapped one at a time.

**How to eat it**

Take one out and eat it in one piece. Do not bite it and put it down. Do not spit. If this is your first paan, eat half and drink water afterwards.

Refrigerate as soon as it reaches you. Best within 14 days of dispatch.

**Who buys it**

People who stopped buying paan because the local shop went tobacco first, and who want the after-meal habit back on the table at home. It is the one paan in most houses that a child or an older person can share, because there is nothing in it to spit and nothing in it to worry about.

**On the price**

A loose paan from a stall costs around ₹15. This box is ₹179 for six. That gap is real and we are not going to talk around it. What you get for it is a named ingredient list, a licensed kitchen, and a paan you can hand to anyone at the table.

If ₹179 is more than you want to spend on a first order, that is fair. This is the entry product in our range and it is the one we would tell you to start with anyway.

**Traditionally taken after meals.**

## SAFETY FLAGS

1. **Three specification fields block listing:** net weight, FSSAI licence number, and the marketed-by entity. Flipkart's food category will not accept the listing without them.
2. **Gluten status is deliberately left as a placeholder.** `CLAUDE.md` states gluten status for Herbal Mukhwas, which is gluten free, and Granola Goodness, which is not. It says nothing about paan. **Do not tick a gluten free box on this listing.** Leaving a field blank is recoverable. A wrong allergen declaration on a food listing is not.
3. **Tier-2 and Tier-3 skew is a real mismatch and it is not a copy problem.** Every customer in the Voice of Customer report is from Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Gurgaon or Kolkata. There is no evidence this product sells outside metros, and a refrigerate-on-arrival product with a 14 day window has more to lose from long transit. See the index.
4. **No named competitor and no Flipkart restricted words.** The price comparison names a category, a street stall, not a brand.
5. **The downsell paragraph is unusual for a marketplace listing** and it is deliberate. It is house voice, it appears on the brand's own pages, and it is the honesty move that a sceptical marketplace shopper responds to. Flag it because it will look like a mistake to anyone used to conventional listing copy.
