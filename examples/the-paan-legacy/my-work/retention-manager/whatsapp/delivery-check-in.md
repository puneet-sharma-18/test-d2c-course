# WhatsApp, Delivery check-in
Fires: 3 days after delivery confirmation. Not 3 days after dispatch.
Segment: all delivered orders. Excludes the exclusions below.
Tone: one question, no sell. This is the brand's own WhatsApp register.
Personalisation tokens: {first_name}, {product}, {city}

## Body

> Hi {first_name}, Puneet here. Your {product} landed three days ago.
> Did it arrive in good condition? Cold, firm, nothing melted at the edges.
> I am asking because {city} shipments are something I track by hand, and I would rather hear it from you than read it in a review.

## The one CTA
A reply. Not a review request. Asking for a review here would be a second ask on top of the first, and this brand asks one thing at a time.

## When not to send
- **An open complaint or refund on this order.** This is the most important exclusion in the set. A cheerful "did it arrive well" landing on someone already waiting for a refund reads as if nobody is reading their messages.
- Customer already replied about this order, positively or otherwise
- Delivery not confirmed by the courier
- No WhatsApp marketing opt-in

## Compliance
WhatsApp category: **Utility**, post-purchase service. No offer, no link. Needs template approval. The `{city}` token is personalisation, not targeting, so it does not change the category.

## Source
`my-work/voice-of-customer/2026-09-09-voc-report.md`, theme 5. Every negative review on record is about condition on arrival, never about the product. Three customers reported a condition problem and all three volunteered that the product itself was still good. This template catches that before it becomes a public review. The register comes from the founder's real WhatsApp reply on a melt complaint: "known weak spot, and we have been slow to solve it".

## SAFETY FLAGS
1. **This template creates work.** It invites replies, and the brand's own standard is answering inside 30 minutes during business hours. Do not switch it on in a week when nobody can staff the inbox. A check-in that goes unanswered is worse than no check-in.
