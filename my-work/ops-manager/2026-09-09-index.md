## Why these two SOPs, and not the other three

Chosen on evidence, per the selection rule.

**Stockout response.** Diwali is 8 November, 60 days out. Corporate gifting runs at roughly 5% of the mix and spikes **8x in October and November**, and the standard corporate bundle is Almond Delight plus Dry Fruit Meetha Paan, so one seasonal surge draws down both gifting SKUs at once. Festival batches ship from 10 October, which is 31 days away. Those two SKUs also carry the thinnest stock cover in the product export. This trigger is more likely to fire than any other in the next 60 days.

**Returns and condition spike.** Every negative review on record in the Voice of Customer report is about condition on arrival or transit time. Not one is about taste, the tobacco-free claim, or the eat-and-swallow promise. That makes condition the entire quality risk surface. The brand profile already quantifies the exposure at 3 to 5 margin points on two SKUs, and the founder has already run this play once by hand, refunding a melted Delhi order and keeping the customer.

**Not written, and why.** Freelancer onboarding: no evidence in any file that hiring is underway. New product launch: no launch in progress. Press or influencer inquiry: no inbound press appears in any source. If any of those three is actually live right now, say so and it takes ten minutes to write.

## Top five reads

1. **The SOP most likely to be needed this week: `sops/stockout-response.md`, and specifically step 1.** The brand profile flags the product export as a partial pull whose absolute volumes are unreliable. That means **there is currently no trustworthy days-of-cover number for any SKU**, 31 days before festival batches ship. Everything else in this SOP runs on a guess until that is fixed. This is the single highest-value hour of ops work available right now.

2. **The template that saves the next half hour: `vendor-kit/purchase-order-confirmation.md`.** Two known input risks make this urgent rather than tidy. Saffron moved in April and the brand profile says Almond Delight's margin may already be 1 to 2 points lower than the sheet says. Granola has a named single-vendor bottleneck with a second vendor still in progress. The festival build has to be ordered in the next few weeks and both price and date need to be in writing before it is.

3. **The returns finding worth investigating: the melt problem may be a transit-time problem wearing a heat costume.** The competitor report found Karachi Bakery quoting 5 to 7 working days for pan-India delivery, which is the category norm. The Voice of Customer report found both Hyderabad reviews clean at 5 stars while Delhi produced the only 3 star, and Kolkata, which is not on the heat list at all, produced a 5 day transit complaint. Sample sizes are tiny and this proves nothing. But if it holds, the fix is courier and routing by SKU, not an insulated box for three named cities, and that is a much cheaper fix.

4. **The SOP needing founder review before it goes to anyone else: `sops/stockout-response.md`, the allocation decision.** Who gets the last stock, confirmed corporate first, then site, then marketplace, is a commercial decision and I have only proposed it. The contribution margin gap of 38 to 42% direct against 28 to 32% on marketplace supports it, but the corporate relationships are the founder's and the call is the founder's.

5. **Nothing is flagged.** All three customer-facing templates were checked against the never-list, the reading level and the FSSAI claim wall. The returns template carries an explicit instruction not to answer a safety question from a template, and to escalate instead.

## What I could not do

- **No returns analysis.** It needs live store data and the connected Shopify store is **Anutilam Rice at anutilam.com**, a different brand entirely. I did not use any of its data, because mixing another store's returns into this brand's operations documentation would be worse than having no analysis. To get a real returns report, connect The Paan Legacy's store.
- **No support-ticket clustering.** The Voice of Customer report contains **zero support tickets**, only reviews and WhatsApp threads. The four WhatsApp threads do cluster usefully into condition complaint and refund, allergen check, corporate bulk enquiry, reorder friction, and a pre-purchase format question. That is a signal, not a pattern, and it should not be treated as a ticket backlog.
- **No escalation playbook and no full vendor kit.** Both are full-run items. The returns SOP currently points at a batch reject email that does not exist yet.
- **No real cover numbers.** I deliberately did not compute days of cover from the product export. The brand profile says its absolute volumes are unreliable, and inventory is a stock level while sales are a channel slice, so any ratio built from the two would look precise and be wrong. The direction is knowable, the number is not, which is why it became step 1 of the SOP rather than a line in this index.
