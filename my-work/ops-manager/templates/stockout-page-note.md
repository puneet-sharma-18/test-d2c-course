# Template, Stockout note for the product page
Use when: a SKU has breached the cover trigger and the page is still live.
Tone: plain, dated, no apology theatre. Class 8 reading level.

## Variant A, restock date known

> Sold out. Back on {date}.
> We roll in small batches and this one went faster than we planned. If you want the next batch held for you, reply on WhatsApp at {whatsapp-number} and we will put your name on it.
> If you do not want to wait, the {alternate-sku} at ₹{alternate-price} is built on the same meetha base.

## Variant B, no date yet

> Sold out. We do not have a date yet and we are not going to invent one.
> Reply on WhatsApp at {whatsapp-number} and we will message you the day it is back. No payment, no subscription.
> The {alternate-sku} at ₹{alternate-price} is in stock today.

## Variant C, festival window

> Sold out for now. The Diwali batches go out from {dispatch-date}.
> If this is for a gift, message us on WhatsApp at {whatsapp-number} before {cutoff-date} and we will reserve boxes against your name.

## Rules
- Give a real date or no date. Never "back soon".
- Always name the in-stock alternative and its price. The downsell is house style, not a fallback.
- Never write that a product is selling fast as a persuasion device. It is a fact here, so state it once and move on.
