## Why it works

The Market Analyst report names one gap above the others: competitors monetise every occasion in the calendar and this brand monetises one. The Voice of Customer report proves the demand already exists, unprompted, for two occasions that were never advertised. This caption does not invent an occasion. It reads back the ones customers already bought for, which makes it credible and makes the ask small.
