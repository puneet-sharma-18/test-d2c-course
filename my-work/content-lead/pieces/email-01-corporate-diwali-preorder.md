Scheduled: 2026-09-11, Friday. Calendar day 2.
Topic: Corporate gifting for Diwali. The pre-order lock, reopened.
Theme it serves: The Market Analyst content gap, corporate Diwali gifting, plus the corporate buyer persona that is missing from `CLAUDE.md` Section 5.
Source: `my-work/market-analyst/2026-09-09-intel-report.md`, "The paid channel you are absent from: corporate Diwali gifting on Meta", "Ship in 30 days: the corporate gifting creative", and "Diwali is 8 November, 60 days out". ## Subject line

Diwali boxes. I need your number by {placeholder: new pre-order lock date}.

## Preview text

## Body

I run these accounts myself, so this is me writing to you and not a team.

Diwali is 8 November. My kitchen ships Diwali batches from 10 October. My pre-order pricing was locked to 31 August. That date has passed and I did not open the window in time. That is my miss, not yours. I am reopening it until {placeholder: new pre-order lock date} and honouring the same price.

Last year an HR lead ordered eighty boxes. She had not seen an ad. She said this:

> "Saw your brand on a colleague's wedding favours."
> corporate buyer, 1 message. Treat as a lead, not a finding.

She had three filters, and every corporate buyer I have spoken to since has had the same three. Employees take these gifts home.

1. Tobacco free. No supari. No zarda.
2. Vegetarian.
3. Safe for a child to eat at the same table.

We clear all three. FSSAI number printed on every box. Manufacturing licence on file and shared on request.

Here is the standard bundle. Almond Delight and Dry Fruit Meetha, dual pack, ₹599. Branded sleeves at ₹40 a box on orders of fifty and up, so your logo goes on the sleeve and not on my paan.

Six paan per box. Hand rolled the morning of dispatch. Best within 14 days of dispatch, so tell me your distribution date and I will work backwards from it.

One thing I will not do. If your headcount is under fifty, do not take the sleeve. It is not worth ₹40 a box at that volume and I would rather say so now.

Puneet.

Almond Delight contains tree nuts. Full allergen list per SKU on request.

## Why it works

This is the only gap where the brand has proven operational capability and zero demand generation. A competitor is running Bangalore-specific corporate Diwali ads this week and this brand has built nothing. The email leads with the missed deadline instead of hiding it, which matches the "admit weakness flat" voice rule, and it uses the one real corporate quote in the data rather than a manufactured testimonial. The downsell at the end, telling a small buyer not to take the sleeve, is the sales policy the brand protects.

## CTA

"Reply with your headcount and your distribution date. I will send back one price and one ship date. No deck."

## SAFETY FLAGS

1. **Invented number risk, avoided but needs a decision.** The original pre-order lock date of 31 August has passed. I did not invent a replacement. `{placeholder: new pre-order lock date}` appears in the subject line and twice in the body and must be filled before send.
2. **Quote sourcing, n=1.** "Saw your brand on a colleague's wedding favours." is verbatim from the corporate persona card in the Voice of Customer report, which itself says "Pulled from: 1 message. n=1. Treat as a lead, not a finding." I have carried that caveat into the piece rather than presenting it as a pattern. If you would rather not quote an n=1 customer at all, strike the quote block and the sentence above it. The email still stands.
3. **"Last year an HR lead ordered eighty boxes."** The Voice of Customer report confirms an 80 box corporate order for Almond Delight and Dry Fruit dual-pack. It does not date it. The input window is 2026-04-09 to 2026-05-09, which is not last Diwali. Either strike "last year" or confirm the date before send.
