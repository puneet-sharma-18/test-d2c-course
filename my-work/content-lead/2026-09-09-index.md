## Top five reads

1. **`pieces/instagram-01-box-makes-the-phone-call.md` ships first, on 10 September.** It is the cheapest thing on the list and it points at the strongest finding in the Voice of Customer report: the gift box recruits its own customers, documented four separate times, and the loop runs on a phone call nobody can attribute. The post does not ask anyone to gift. It reads the loop back to the person who starts it. Carries one flag on the CTA.

2. **Closest to the number one customer theme: `pieces/instagram-02-sunday-table.md`, 13 September.** Theme 3, eaten at a table never alone, is 8 of 27 and the largest theme in the set. This is a Sunday post that names the exact slot after Sunday lunch and lands tobacco-free as a permission slip rather than as a benefit, which is what the customers actually say it is. `pieces/email-02-how-to-eat-it.md` serves the same theme on the delivery-day trigger, where the 30% repeat rate makes it worth the most.

3. **Attacks the competitive gap: `pieces/email-01-corporate-diwali-preorder.md`, 11 September.** The Market Analyst report's one instruction was "ship in 30 days: the corporate gifting creative". Thirteen of twenty five ads in that Ad Library search are live corporate Diwali campaigns, one targeting Bangalore companies by name, and this brand has zero creative built against a motion it already runs by hand and has already won an eighty box order through. The email leads by admitting the 31 August pre-order deadline was missed. It carries three flags and one of them, the replacement lock date, blocks send.

4. **Biggest expected lift: `marketplace/amazon-aplus-01-almond-delight-paan.md`.** Not because A+ copy is magic, but because there is no shelf at all. Anjali Mukhwas ranks through Amazon and Flipkart rather than a domain, and Karachi Bakery has marketplace presence this brand does not. Almond Delight is the right SKU to open with: rank 1 on revenue and conversion, 64% margin, and 5 of 5 five-star reviews with no complaint of any kind in the customer data. The hero line is `Tobacco-free paan. Eat, do not spit.` at 36 characters, and backend keyword 1 is the exact phrase that returned zero active ads in the Ad Library.

5. **Carrying the most serious flags: `marketplace/flipkart-01-almond-delight-paan.md`, eight of them.** Two are hard blocks. The gluten-free tick box must stay empty, because `CLAUDE.md` states gluten status only for Herbal Mukhwas and Granola Goodness and says nothing about Almond Delight, and ticking it without a test report turns a description into a compliance claim. Net weight is missing and Flipkart requires it, which matters more here than anywhere else because rivals on that shelf compete in grams. Also read flag 1 on both listings: "Kashmiri grade saffron, three threads per paan", "rose petals from the Pushkar belt" and "240gsm uncoated kraft" appear in `CLAUDE.md` Section 7 only as examples of a voice rule, never as product data, so I kept them off both pages. If they are true they are the strongest lines available and belong back in.

## What I didn't do

- **No exact festival dates.** Only Diwali on 8 November is sourced, from the Market Analyst report. Ganesh Chaturthi, Navratri, Dussehra and Karva Chauth appear in the calendar as occasions and never as dates. Calendar days 3, 20 and 21 carry `{placeholder: confirm 2026 dates}` and cannot be scheduled until those are filled.
- **No Herbal Mukhwas content, in the calendar or in the pieces.** Both reports independently say the same thing and the Voice of Customer report says it flatly: "The fix is 300g or a lower price, not better copy." Two of two mukhwas reviewers complained about grams while praising the roasting, and the Market Analyst report found live rivals selling 400g and 5x100g gift packs plus an explicit "Mouth Fresheners Under ₹300" anchor in the same feed. Writing copy against that is writing copy against arithmetic. Herbal Mukhwas is also the highest FSSAI risk SKU in the file, tagged `digestion` and named `Herbal`, with a competitor advertising "Digestive Mouth Freshener" that this brand cannot match. I would rather build nothing for it than build something that drifts over the claim wall. It gets a slot the week after the 200g versus 300g decision is made.
- **No Masala Cranberry content, and I think that is worth a second look.** `CLAUDE.md` calls it last on every metric and asks fix or retire. The Voice of Customer report disagrees: 3 reviews at 4.67, two of the three describing a repeat purchase, and it names the real problem as a 1.9% conversion rate, which is a page problem rather than a recipe problem. That is a product-page job, not a calendar job, so I left it out rather than spend a social slot on it. It belongs in the Session 7 product-page work.
- **No summer shipping reassurance creative,** even though `CLAUDE.md` ranks it the number one creative gap by cost. The melt window is May to August and this calendar runs 10 September to 9 October. Building it now would be building it eight months early. The Voice of Customer report also reframes it as possibly a transit-time problem that looks like a heat problem, since both Hyderabad reviews are clean five stars and the only melt complaint is Delhi, so the brief may change before the creative is needed.
- **No competitive copy of any kind.** No piece names Bombay Sweet Shop, Anjali Mukhwas, Karachi Bakery, Paantaara, The Betel Leaf Co, Open Secret or TagZ. `CLAUDE.md` Section 6 forbids writing against Open Secret and TagZ, and the Market Analyst report marks every competitor price unverified with three storefronts blocked by the egress proxy. Anything comparative would have been unsourced. The only comparison anywhere in the output is against the loose street paan at ₹15, which both the brand profile and the customer data support.
- **No health claim, and no attempt to get near one.** The only after-meal language anywhere in these seven files is "traditionally taken after meals", used verbatim. Nothing about digestion, gut health, immunity or detox appears in any file except inside the safety flags telling you not to write it.
- **No net weight, FSSAI licence number, support contact or replacement pre-order lock date.** Four facts I do not have. Every one is a marked placeholder in the file that needs it rather than a number I made up.
- **No reel scripts, blog posts or newsletter sections.** They are on the calendar as days 3, 6, 15, 16, 17, 21, 24, 26, 12 and 28, and they were not drafted because this was the focused run.
