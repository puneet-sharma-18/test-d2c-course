## Description

The paan sold at most shops is built to be spat out. This one is built to be eaten.

That is the whole product, and it is the question people ask before they pay. Is this spit-out paan or eat-and-swallow. It is eat-and-swallow. No tobacco, no supari, no zarda.

**What is in it**

Almond. Saffron. Rose. Fennel. Cardamom. Betel leaf, and the leaf is leaf, not garnish. Every one of them is named because every one of them is in there. The almond is not an almond essence. It is an almond.

Vegetarian. Hand rolled in a Mumbai kitchen on the morning your box is dispatched, so it is not sitting in a warehouse waiting for you.

**What is in the box**

Six paan, wrapped one by one, in a kraft box. Two paans per person is the right number, so a box of six is three people at one table. Do not treat it as six days of paan for one person. It is not built for that and it will not taste right on day nine.

Refrigerate it the moment it reaches you. Not the freezer. Best within 14 days of dispatch. Traditionally taken after meals.

**Who eats it**

Not one person alone. Our customers keep telling us who else was at the table.
