## The bias behind the plan

The Market Analyst line I am writing against: **"The content gap: occasions that are not Diwali."** Bombay Sweet Shop is running at least 13 live creatives and not one is Diwali. The brand's own customers already bought for a housewarming and for Akshaya Tritiya without being asked. So eight days in this calendar name an occasion that is not Diwali.

The Voice of Customer line I am writing against: **"It is eaten at a table, never alone", 8 of 27, the largest theme in the set.** Every piece below that serves it is written for two people at a table, not one person with a phone.

## What the calendar deliberately does not do

- **No Diwali-only month.** Eight of thirty days name an occasion that is not Diwali, because that is the gap the Market Analyst found and the demand the Voice of Customer report already recorded.
- **No competitive copy against Open Secret or TagZ.** `CLAUDE.md` Section 6 forbids it, and the Market Analyst report notes TagZ was acquired in a distress sale, so a comparison would also be stale.
- **No Paantaara callout.** Paantaara is live on the exact post-meal positioning as of 9 September, but the Market Analyst report gives ad IDs and no product, price or claim I could fairly compare against. Comparative copy would be unsourced.
- **No mukhwas pack-size campaign.** Both the Voice of Customer report and the Market Analyst report say the same thing: copy cannot win an argument about grams. Herbal Mukhwas gets no calendar slot until the 200g versus 300g decision is made.
- **No named festival dates other than Diwali on 8 November,** which the Market Analyst report sources. Ganesh Chaturthi, Navratri, Dussehra and Karva Chauth appear as occasions, not as dates. Fill `{placeholder: confirm 2026 dates}` before scheduling days 3, 20 and 21.
