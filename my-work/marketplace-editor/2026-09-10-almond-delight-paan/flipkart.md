## Description, 300 to 500 words

**The paan we send when we want to be remembered.**

Almond Delight is not the everyday paan. It is the one that goes to a wedding, or to your in-laws instead of a box of sweets. If you have never bought paan as a gift before, this is the one to start with.

**Made the morning it ships**

We hand roll this in a small kitchen in Mumbai on the day it is dispatched. It is not made in advance and stored in a warehouse. Every box carries a packing date so you can check it yourself. Best within 14 days of that date. Refrigerate as soon as it arrives.

**What is in it**

Almond, slivered by hand. Saffron. Rose. Gulkand. Built on our meetha base and wrapped in a betel leaf you eat.

The almond is an almond, not almond essence. The saffron is saffron, not a saffron colour. Everything is printed on the box. Contains tree nuts.

**No tobacco. No supari. No zarda. Vegetarian.**

You eat the whole thing, leaf and all. Nothing to spit and nothing to worry about handing to an older person or a child at the same table.

**How to eat it**

One at a time, whole. Do not bite it and put it down. Two per person is the right serving, so a box of six is three people after a meal.

**On the price**

A loose paan from a stall costs around ₹15. This is ₹299 for six. We are not going to pretend that gap is not there. What it buys is a named ingredient list, a licensed kitchen, and a box that already looks like a gift when it arrives.

If ₹299 is more than you want to spend on a first order, buy our Meetha Paan at ₹179 instead. It is the same base and it is where most people start.

**Traditionally taken after meals.**
