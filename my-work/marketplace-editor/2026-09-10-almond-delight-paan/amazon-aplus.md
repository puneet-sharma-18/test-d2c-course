## Bullets, 5, 250 characters each

1. **MADE THE MORNING IT SHIPS.** Hand-rolled in a Mumbai kitchen and dispatched the same day, not held in a warehouse. Every box carries a packing date. Best within 14 days of dispatch, refrigerate on arrival. This is fresh food, not shelf stock.

2. **EAT IT, DO NOT SPIT IT.** No tobacco, no supari, no zarda. You eat the whole paan, betel leaf included. This is the question buyers ask before ordering, so it is answered here rather than buried in the description.

3. **ALMOND, SAFFRON AND ROSE, NAMED NOT IMPLIED.** Hand-slivered almond, saffron, rose, gulkand, on a meetha base. The almond is an almond, not almond essence. Everything in the paan is printed on the box. Contains tree nuts.

4. **BOX OF 6, WRAPPED ONE BY ONE, GIFT READY AS IT SHIPS.** Silver-foil-stamped kraft box, no extra wrapping needed. Two per person is a serving, so one box serves three at a table. Sent as a wedding, Diwali or thank-you gift.

5. **FSSAI LICENSED KITCHEN, LICENCE NUMBER ON EVERY BOX.** Manufacturing licence on file and shared on request. Vegetarian. Allergens: almond and other tree nuts, cardamom, rose, fennel. Traditionally taken after meals.

## Module 1, image and body, 250 words

**Image:** the open box, top down, six wrapped paan, one unwrapped on a plain plate beside it, saffron threads visible in the fold. Second frame, a hand slivering almonds.

**Headline:** Rolled this morning. Not made to stock.

**Body**

Two things a marketplace shopper cannot check from a photograph, so we will state them.

**When it was made.** This is hand-rolled on the morning it is dispatched, in a small Mumbai kitchen, in batches. It is not produced in advance and warehoused. Every box carries a packing date. Best within 14 days of that date, and it needs refrigerating as soon as it reaches you.

**What is actually in it.** Almond, slivered by hand. Saffron. Rose. Gulkand. On the meetha base. The almond is not almond essence, it is an almond. The saffron is not a saffron colour, it is saffron. All of it is printed on the box so you can check it against what you are eating.

No tobacco. No supari. No zarda. Vegetarian. Contains tree nuts.

**How to eat it.** Take one out and eat it in one piece. Do not bite it and put it down. Do not spit. The leaf is meant to be swallowed. Two per person is the right number.

**On the price.** A loose paan from a stall is around ₹15. This is ₹299 for six. That gap is real. What it buys is a named ingredient list, an FSSAI licensed kitchen, and a box you can hand to someone without wrapping it first.

Traditionally taken after meals.

## SAFETY FLAGS

1. **Net weight and FSSAI licence number are still missing.** Both were flagged on the Content Lead's first pass and neither has been supplied. They block the listing.
2. **"Silver-foil-stamped kraft box" and the thin silver leaf** come from the live product page. The live page also mentions silver leaf on "the special-occasion ones", which implies it is not on every box. **Confirm whether the silver leaf ships as standard before it appears in a bullet.** If it is conditional, cut it.
3. **Tree nut allergen is stated three times, in a bullet, the module body and the story.** That is deliberate for a marketplace listing and should not be trimmed for length.
4. **The refrigeration row in the comparison chart is a deliberate negative.** Do not remove it to make the chart look better. It is there to prevent a condition complaint becoming a one-star review.
5. **No Amazon restricted words, no named competitor, no health claim.** Only "traditionally taken after meals".
6. **Gifting keywords assume you will actually be listed before Diwali on 8 November.** If the Amazon launch slips past mid-October those terms are dead weight for a year.
