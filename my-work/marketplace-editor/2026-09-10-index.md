## Read this before you list anything

**This brand is not on Amazon or Flipkart.** Nothing here is a rewrite of a live listing. There is no marketplace conversion data, no Seller Central or Seller Hub export, and no listing reviews to learn from. These are launch listings.

**The "marketplace reviews" in your Voice of Customer report are Zomato and Swiggy.** That is food delivery, in-city, fast. An Amazon or Flipkart shopper is a third persona you have no data on at all. Where I used the marketplace-source reviews, it was for what they reveal about a faster, more sceptical reader, not as evidence about Amazon behaviour.

**The structural problem, which matters more than any copy in this folder.**

Your two highest-volume products are hand-rolled perishables with a 14 day window and a refrigerate-on-arrival instruction. Amazon and Flipkart fulfilment is warehousing plus multi-day transit with no cold chain. Your own data already says what happens when this product spends too long in transit: every negative review you have is about condition on arrival, and the only 3 star in the set is a melted summer shipment. The Market Analyst report found the category norm for pan-India marketplace delivery is 5 to 7 working days, which is half your shelf life spent in a van.

So the two SKUs the order data picks are the two worst suited to the channel.

**The SKU that is actually marketplace-native is Herbal Mukhwas.** Shelf stable, no refrigeration, no 14 day clock, and it is the exact category where your direct rival is already on Amazon and Flipkart and you are not. It is also the SKU blocked by the 200g against 300g decision.

That decision has now blocked three separate pieces of work: the Content Lead wrote no mukhwas content, the storefront rewrite says copy cannot close a format gap, and marketplace entry points at mukhwas as the right first listing. **It is the highest-leverage open decision in the business right now, and it is a packaging decision, not a marketing one.**

## Products picked, and why

Top two by marketplace orders. Your marketplaces today are Zomato and Swiggy, so I used units over 90 days as the proxy: Meetha Paan at 1,548 and Almond Delight at 1,144. Almond Delight already had a Content Lead first pass, so that one is a genuine rewrite. Meetha Paan is new.

## Top five reads

1. **The Amazon rewrite most likely to lift conversion: Almond Delight.** The first pass led with the story. This one leads with the two things a marketplace shopper cannot verify from a photo and is actually suspicious about, when it was made and what is really in it. Packing date and the freshness argument move to the first bullet and the top of module 1. The comparison chart also carries a deliberate negative, "needs refrigeration, yes", because telling a sceptical shopper the inconvenient thing before they buy is what stops a one-star review afterwards.

2. **The Flipkart listing to look at first: Meetha Paan.** At ₹179 it is the entry price in your range, and Flipkart is the more price-led platform of the two. If any product of yours belongs there, it is this one. The specification table is the part that matters, not the description.

3. **The competitor move worth copying this week: get on the marketplace shelf in the mukhwas category.** The Market Analyst report found Anjali Mukhwas selling roughly 400g through Amazon and Flipkart with zero Meta spend, letting the marketplaces do the ranking. That is a cheap distribution model you are not using, in the one category where your product does not fight the fulfilment.

4. **The listing needing a voice check: Almond Delight on Flipkart.** It downsells to Meetha Paan inside the description, which is house voice and correct on your own site. On a marketplace it sends a shopper to a product that may not be listed yet. Read that paragraph and decide.

5. **Flagged and blocking, on all four listings: net weight, FSSAI licence number, and the marketed-by entity.** None of the three appears in any file in this repo. No food listing goes live on either platform without them. Net weight and FSSAI were already flagged on the Content Lead's first pass yesterday and are still outstanding.

## The gluten field, deliberately left blank

Every one of these four listings leaves the gluten specification as a placeholder. `CLAUDE.md` states gluten status for Herbal Mukhwas, which is gluten free, and for Granola Goodness, which is not. It says nothing about paan. Do not tick that box to complete a form. A blank field is recoverable, a wrong allergen declaration on a food listing is not.
