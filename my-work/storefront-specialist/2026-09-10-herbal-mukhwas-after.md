## Description

This is the mukhwas we roast ourselves, in the same kitchen as the paan, in batches small enough that one person watches the pan.

Most mukhwas is bought in by the sack and re-bagged. Ours is not. That is the whole difference and it is the only reason to pay what we ask.

**Why 200g and not 400g.**

A 400g bag from a sweet shop costs about ₹{competitor-price}. Ours is 200g at ₹219. You are paying roughly {multiple} times as much per gram, and you should know that before you buy rather than after.

Here is what the money buys. The seeds are roasted, not warmed. Roasting is what turns fennel from grassy to sweet, and it is the step that gets skipped when you are doing forty kilos at a time. The ingredients are named on the pack, so you can count them. There is no supari, which is what most cheap mukhwas is padded with, because supari is heavy, cheap and does the work of filling a bag.

We sell 200g because that is roughly six weeks of use for one house, and mukhwas that sits open past that stops tasting roasted and starts tasting stale. A bigger bag is a worse product by the time you reach the bottom of it.

If you want to spend less, buy the loose mukhwas at your sweet shop. It is honest food and it costs a fraction of this. Buy ours when you want to know what is in it.

**How to use it.** A small spoon after a meal. That is the whole instruction. It has been taken that way for a very long time and we are not going to tell you it does anything more than that.

## SAFETY FLAGS

1. **Two placeholders block publishing.** `{competitor-price}` and `{multiple}` carry the whole price-gap argument. The Market Analyst report marks every competitor price as **unverified**, because Flipkart and the competitor storefronts are proxy-blocked. Do not fill these from memory. Check one real 400g pack, then write the number.
2. **`{servings-per-pack}` and `{cost-per-serving}` are invented structure, not invented numbers.** Nothing in any file states servings per pack. Either count it or delete bullet 4.
3. **`{shelf-life}` is not stated anywhere for this SKU.** The 14 day figure in `CLAUDE.md` is for paan, not mukhwas. Do not reuse it.
4. **Deliberate claim-wall restraint, please do not "improve" this.** The product export tags this SKU `digestion` and the name contains the word Herbal. The line "It has been taken that way for a very long time and we are not going to tell you it does anything more than that" is doing compliance work. A rival is advertising "Digestive Mouth Freshener" and you cannot match it. Any edit that adds a digestion, gut, immunity or detox benefit crosses the FSSAI wall.
5. **Allergen line needs confirming.** "Made in a kitchen that also handles tree nuts and oats" is inferred from the Almond Delight and Granola specifications, not stated for this SKU. It is the safer direction, but confirm it is true before printing it.
6. **The bigger-pack FAQ answer commits you to nothing but implies motion.** If the 200g against 300g decision has already been made either way, replace it with the real answer.
