## Description

We make paan. This is the one thing on the site that is not paan, so it is worth saying plainly what it is.

It is granola. You eat it at breakfast with milk or curd, or by the handful in the afternoon. That is it. It is not a mouth freshener and you do not eat it after a meal.

**Why a paan brand makes granola.**

Because it is the same job. Roasting. The kitchen that roasts fennel and cardamom for the mukhwas is the kitchen that roasts oats and nuts for this, in the same small batches, watched by the same people. We are not a granola company that bought a recipe. We are a roasting kitchen that makes two things.

**What actually happens when people buy it.**

They buy this first, because it is the easy one to try, and then they buy the paan. We have watched it enough times to stop pretending it is a coincidence. One customer put it better than we could: she started this for her kids' breakfast, ended up eating it herself, and now orders the granola and the Meetha Paan together.

So if you are here and you have never eaten our paan, this is a reasonable place to start. And if you like it, the Meetha Paan at ₹179 is the thing we actually built the kitchen for.

**One honest note about summer.**

It can arrive soft if it has been sitting in a hot van. It is still fine to eat, and it firms up if you spread it on a tray for ten minutes. If yours arrives soft enough to bother you, message us and we will sort it out. We would rather you told us.

## FAQ

**Is this gluten free?**
No. It contains oats, and the oats we use are not certified gluten free. If you are buying for someone with celiac disease, do not buy this. The Herbal Mukhwas is gluten free.

**Is it very sweet?**
{sweetness-answer}. Ask us on WhatsApp if you want the sugar figure before you order.

**Can children eat it?**
It is breakfast granola, so yes, unless there is a nut or oat allergy. Check the ingredient list on the pack.

**Do I eat this after a meal like the paan?**
No. This is breakfast or a snack. The paan is the after-meal one.

**Why did mine arrive soft?**
Heat in transit, usually. It is safe to eat. Spread it on a tray for ten minutes and it comes back. Tell us if it happens, because we are tracking which lanes it happens on.
