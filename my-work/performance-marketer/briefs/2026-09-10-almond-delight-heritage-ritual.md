# Creative Brief, Almond Delight heritage ritual relight
Date: 2026-09-10
Surface: Meta static, single image. Feed and Stories.
Format: 1080 x 1080 for feed, 1080 x 1920 safe-area crop for Stories. Shoot 4:5 native so both crops come from one frame. JPG or PNG, sRGB.

## The angle in one sentence

The paan your grandmother made on Sundays, made properly, eaten at a table with other people, with nothing to spit out.

## Why now

This is the best performing creative in the account and it has not run for 22 days. `my-work/growth-analyst/2026-09-10-brief.md` records zero Meta spend in the last 7 days and ₹1,615.56 across 90 days, all of it in the week ending 19 August on a mukhwas prospecting campaign. The heritage ritual angle is dark.

Diwali is 8 November, 59 days out, and `my-work/market-analyst/2026-09-09-intel-report.md` found 13 of 25 ads in a corporate Diwali search already live on 8 and 9 September. This is the cheapest way back into the auction, because it is a proven angle rather than a new one.

The strongest supporting evidence is in `my-work/voice-of-customer/2026-09-09-voc-report.md`, theme 3: **8 of 27 customer messages name another person in the house.** That is the largest theme in the set and the current creative does not show it.

## Audience

- **Who:** the ritual restorer. Mumbai, Pune and Bengaluru, 32 to 48, household income proxy 15L+. The stated top audience for this exact creative in `CLAUDE.md` Section 7.
- **What they care about:** putting an after-meal habit back on a table other people sit at. Not indulgence, not a treat for themselves. Two of the three quotes in the customer research name a mother or a mother in law.
- **What stops them buying:** one question, asked before they pay. Is this spit-out paan or eat-and-swallow. Five of 27 messages turn on it, and one customer asked it on WhatsApp before ordering.

## The three things the visual must contain

1. **One unwrapped paan, whole, where the fold reads clearly as eat-in-one-piece.** Almond slivers and saffron threads must be individually visible. The named ingredients are the proof, so if a viewer cannot count them the shot has failed.
2. **A second pair of hands.** This is the change from the current creative and it is the whole point. The product is never eaten alone. Two people in frame, one offering and one receiving.
3. **Ordinary domestic daylight on an ordinary table.** The argument that you do not spit this out is made by the setting, not by the copy. A dining table at home does the work that a caption cannot.

## Tone

- **Three always-phrases from the voice rules:** "Eat, do not spit." / "Hand-rolled this morning." / "No tobacco. No supari."
- **Three never-words:** gourmet, premium, authentic.
- **Mood in one line:** a Sunday afternoon in a house where lunch has just finished and nobody has got up yet.
- **Reading level for any text on the visual:** Class 8. Short declaratives. No sentence longer than nine words.

## What it must not show

From the anti-positioning in `CLAUDE.md` Section 3:
- Nothing that argues on price. No discount flash, no strike-through, no "starting at".
- No stock imagery. If it looks licensed, it is wrong.
- No claim beyond "traditionally taken after meals". Nothing about digestion, gut health, immunity or freshness benefits.

The category's visual clichés, named specifically, because this category has a very loud house style and the brand exists to reject it:
- **Anything that reads as a paan masala or gutkha ad.** Gold, maroon, bombast, a celebrity holding a pouch. This is the dominant visual language of the category and it is the exact thing to run away from.
- The paan-wala stall. Red-stained counter, steel trays, a man in a vest folding at speed.
- Any red staining anywhere, on anything.
- Betel leaf arranged on a brass thali with marigolds and diyas. The generic Indian festive stock composition.
- Silver varq catching a studio highlight as the hero of the frame.
- Hands in heavy bridal jewellery.
- Soft-focus incense smoke, hanging dupattas, temple bells.
- Hyper-saturated warm grading. The grade should look like a window, not like a filter.

## Craft references

Neither is a competitor. Both are chosen for a specific element, not for a look to copy.

- **Paper Boat, for how they handle memory without nostalgia kitsch.** Study how they make an Indian childhood specific rather than generic, and how restrained their colour is while still feeling warm. They sell beverages, so there is no category overlap at all.
- **Aesop, for ingredient-forward product photography.** Study how a named ingredient becomes the subject of the frame rather than a caption underneath it. That is the visual equivalent of "the almond is not almond essence, it is an almond".

## Image prompt

> An overhead photograph, shot straight down from about a metre above a worn wooden dining table in an Indian home, of one hand offering a small plain steel plate holding a single unwrapped paan while a second, older pair of hands reaches in from the opposite edge of the frame to take it. The paan is folded in a fresh green betel leaf, its edges soft and slightly translucent, with hand-slivered almond and three visible saffron threads catching light at the open fold. Beside the plate, slightly out of the main focus, sits an open kraft box with a silver foil stamp, five remaining paan wrapped individually in clear cellophane. The light is late afternoon daylight from a window to the left, soft and directional, with real shadows and no fill, the kind of light a phone camera would find in a Mumbai flat at four in the afternoon. Composition is off-centre, the plate sitting slightly right of middle with generous empty table at the top of the frame for the headline. Colour is muted and true: green leaf, warm brown wood, dull steel, one small saffron accent. The mood is unhurried and domestic, a meal that finished ten minutes ago, two generations still at the table.

**Negative prompt:** no red or maroon staining anywhere, no spitting, no stall or street vendor setting, no man in a vest, no brass thali, no marigolds, no diyas, no incense smoke, no temple bells, no bridal jewellery, no gold foil backdrops, no silk or velvet, no celebrity, no saturated orange grading, no studio seamless backdrop, no stock-photo smiling to camera, no faces, no text, no logos, no discount badge, no pouch or sachet packaging, no paan masala tin, no soft-focus vignette.

## Notes for whoever shoots this

- **Faces are deliberately out of frame.** Two pairs of hands carry the multi-generation story without casting, and without the shot reading as licensed stock. Age difference has to be legible in the hands alone, so cast for that.
- **The empty table at the top is for the headline.** Do not fill it.
- **Shoot the box slightly open, not styled open.** The Voice of Customer report has a customer saying "the packaging itself feels like a gift", so the box earns its place in frame, but the paan is the subject.
- Every product detail above comes from `CLAUDE.md` Section 4 and the live Almond Delight product page. The table, the steel plate and the window light are art direction and are not brand facts, so change them freely if the location gives you something better.
