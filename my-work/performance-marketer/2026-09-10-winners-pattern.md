## The one measured result in the whole account

"Tobacco-free" beats "natural" and "pure" by 1.4x. That is the only A/B result on file. It sets the control word for every ad in this run, and it is why "natural" and "pure" are on my never-list alongside the four banned adjectives.

## How every variant in this run uses the pattern

Each ad either **matches** the pattern, which is the safer test, or **breaks it on exactly one axis**, which is a probe. Each ad's "why this works" line names which one and which axis. The axes in play across this run: hook style, format, copy length, offer framing, the family member named, the occasion named, the persona, and geography. No ad in this run differs from the control on more than one.

The control in both angles is the pattern ad: SKU name plus tobacco-free negation, static image. A4-1 is the control for angle 4 and it is also the relight the Growth Analyst brief asks for.
