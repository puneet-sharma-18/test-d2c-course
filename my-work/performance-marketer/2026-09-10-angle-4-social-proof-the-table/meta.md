## SAFETY FLAGS

Seven checks run on all five ads. Nothing was silently fixed.

1. **Format is my choice, not a learned pattern.** The winners pattern records angle names and one campaign name, never a creative format. "Static" and "reel" here are decisions, not inheritance. If the founder has old creative files, one look at them beats this whole assumption.
2. **The attribution on A4-2 is from a different file than the quote.** The sentence is verbatim in the VoC report, where it is printed unattributed. "R, Gurgaon" comes from `CLAUDE.md` S5. Both are the brand's own records, but they are two sources stitched together. Confirm R agreed to be quoted publicly before this runs, or drop the initial and the city and run the sentence alone.
3. **Every quote in this file is four months old.** The VoC set runs 2026-04-09 to 2026-05-09 and this is September. The report also warns the set is positively skewed with no 1 or 2 star review in it. These quotes are true and they are spring. Nothing here is disproved by that, but a testimonial ad is the one format where a stale set matters most.
4. **Prices are from a sheet last updated March 2026.** ₹179 and ₹299 come from `CLAUDE.md` S4, which says saffron moved up in April and Almond Delight may run 1 to 2 margin points lower. A price in a live ad is a promise. Re-check both before spend.
5. **No child appears in any ad in this angle, deliberately.** The strongest quote in Theme 3 is "The kids can even have a small bite", and Almond Delight declares tree nuts. Putting a child in a tree-nut ad is a risk the copy gain does not cover. The Content Lead flagged the same thing on the organic Sunday post. Founder's call, not mine.
6. **Allergens are not in the ad copy and cannot fit.** Almond Delight declares tree nuts, cardamom, rose and fennel. At 30 characters of description there is no room. The landing page has to carry the declaration, and the ad has to point at a page that does.
7. **Account status blocks the launch, not the copy.** `my-work/growth-analyst/2026-09-10-brief.md` reports Meta account 1145209407174523 as DISABLED, flagged for unusual activity with all ads paused. Launch into 888048036093502, which is active and carries the payment method. Confirm which account before anything is uploaded.
8. **No budget or ROAS number appears in this file.** The only live benchmark in the account is 62 clicks at ₹26.06 CPC from ₹1,615.56 of spend. That is too thin to plan against, so the budget line is `{placeholder}` rather than a number I made up.

Checks that came back clean: no word from the never-list and none of the two deprecated words, no health claim of any kind, no invented number, every customer quote string-matched verbatim against the VoC report, no competitor named, no comparative claim, no em dash.
