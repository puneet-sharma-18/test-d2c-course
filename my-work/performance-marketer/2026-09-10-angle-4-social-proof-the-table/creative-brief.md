# Creative Brief, The Sunday Table
Date: 2026-09-10
Surface: Meta feed and Instagram feed, static. One 15 second vertical video as a secondary build.
Format: 1080x1080 and 1080x1350 static, JPG or PNG. Video 1080x1920, 15 seconds, ambient sound, no music bed.

## The angle in one sentence

Show the second plate, because nobody eats this alone.

## Why now

Voice of Customer Theme 3, "It is eaten at a table, never alone", is **8 of 27, the largest theme in the set**, and eight separate customers name another person in the house. The report's read is that tobacco-free is not a benefit to these people, it is a permission slip that lets a mother-in-law or a child join in. Source: `my-work/voice-of-customer/2026-09-09-voc-report.md`.

Second reason, and it is the urgent one. `my-work/growth-analyst/2026-09-10-brief.md` reports the Meta account dark for 22 days, with the heritage ritual creative on Almond Delight, the best ROAS angle in the account, not running at all. This brief is the relight. Diwali is 8 November, 59 days out, and the brand's own gifting window opens 5 October, so this creative has roughly three weeks to gather learning before the season it has to carry.

Third, from the winners pattern: **no ad this account has ever run used a customer's own sentence as the hook.** Every angle name on file is a marketer's phrase. That is the gap this brief probes.

## Audience

- **Who:** the Sunday table restorer. 35 to 50, metro household, Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru, Delhi, Hyderabad. Pulled from 10 messages in the VoC report. They had stopped buying paan because the local shop is tobacco-first.
- **What they care about:** putting an after-meal ritual back on a table other people sit at. Not a snack, a slot in the week. Sunday lunch and the evening after dinner.
- **What stops them buying:** one question, asked out loud before money moves. "Hi, is this spit-out paan or eat-and-swallow?" VoC Theme 1, 5 of 27. Price is not the blocker. The VoC report is flat about it: price is an objection that dies on contact with the product.

**Note on the age band.** `CLAUDE.md` targets 32 to 48. The VoC report found not one age signal in 27 messages and asks for that band to be labelled an ad-targeting assumption rather than a customer fact. Treat it as a targeting input, never as a casting instruction.

## The three things the visual must contain

1. **Two settings, never one.** Two plates, two glasses, two places at the table. The second person does not need to be in frame. Their plate does.
2. **One paan, unwrapped, at actual size,** on a plain steel plate. Named ingredients visible as ingredients: rose petals, saffron threads, the almond. Loose beside the paan, never arranged as decoration.
3. **Text that carries the negation.** "Tobacco-free" or "Eat, do not spit." set in plain type. The claim is what the category is not, which is the one hook style with a measured result behind it.

## Tone

- **Three always-words from the voice rules:** name the thing rather than adjective it. Negate, then assert. Family is the authority, never the brand.
- **Three never-words:** gourmet, premium, authentic. Plus artisanal, elevate, unlock, and the two deprecated ones, natural and pure, which tested 1.4x worse than tobacco-free.
- **Mood in one line:** a home kitchen at four in the afternoon, not a restaurant at eight in the evening.
- **Reading level, for any text on the visual:** Class 8. Short words, concrete nouns, no subordinate clauses.

## What it must not show

From the anti-positioning:

- **No luxury signalling.** No gold foil, no marble, no velvet, no deep jewel-tone backdrop, no serif script. The brand bans the words that go with that look, and the look is the same claim made visually.
- **No price-competing cue.** No discount flash, no "starting at", no strike-through. `CLAUDE.md` S3 says the gap gets said out loud, never closed.

From the category's visual clichés, named for paan specifically:

- **No betel leaf used as garnish.** The brand's own paired refrain is "The leaf is leaf, not garnish." A leaf tucked under a plate as decoration contradicts the line.
- **No red stain, no spitting, no mouth in motion, no paan-shop counter with tobacco tins or zarda pouches, no supari.** The entire brand is the absence of these.
- **No silver varq glitter shot, no rotating hero on a turntable, no confetti, no sparkler.** These are the festival-food defaults and they read as every other box.
- **No child eating the product.** Almond Delight declares tree nuts. This is the founder's call, and the Content Lead flagged the same thing on the organic Sunday post.
- **No stock family.** If people appear, hands only, and real ones.

## Craft references

- **Paper Boat**, for how they photograph a remembered thing rather than a product. Study the restraint in their still frames, where the object sits in ordinary domestic light and the memory does the work. Not a competitor, not in this category.
- **Nicobar**, for typography discipline and product-first composition on a plain ground. Study how small they let the type be. Not a competitor, not in food.

## Image prompt

A tight overhead photograph of a single unwrapped paan resting on a plain steel plate on a worn wooden dining table, shot straight down from directly above, with a second empty steel plate entering the frame at the edge to show a place set for another person. The paan is a folded green betel leaf parcel at actual size, hand-rolled and slightly irregular, with a few loose rose petals and three saffron threads set on the bare wood beside the plate as ingredients rather than as decoration, and one whole almond nearby. Soft late-afternoon daylight from a window at the left, unfiltered and directional, with gentle shadows and no artificial fill, on an Indian home kitchen table rather than a studio surface. Composition is centred and calm with generous empty wood at the top of the frame for a short line of plain type, colours limited to green leaf, warm brown wood, brushed steel and kraft paper brown. Mood is domestic, quiet and unstyled, the look of a real table between meals.

**Negative prompt:** no betel leaf used as garnish or tucked under the plate, no red spit stain, no mouth, no spitting, no paan-shop counter, no tobacco tins, no zarda pouch, no supari nuts, no silver varq glitter, no gold foil, no marble surface, no velvet, no jewel-tone backdrop, no serif script or English calligraphy, no confetti, no sparklers, no diyas, no rotating turntable hero, no studio seamless background, no stock family, no children, no faces, no discount badge, no strike-through price, no competitor packaging, no text claiming any health, digestion, immunity or detox benefit.
