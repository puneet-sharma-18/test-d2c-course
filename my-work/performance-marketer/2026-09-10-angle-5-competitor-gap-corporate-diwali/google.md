## SAFETY FLAGS

1. **BLOCKING. ₹599 appears in G5-A and G5-B and the pre-order lock expired 31 August.** `CLAUDE.md` S7 says corporate pre-order pricing locks 31 August, and that date is 10 days past. Set a new lock date or replace the price with `{placeholder}`. Same block as the corporate ads and as the Content Lead's corporate email.
2. **"Kid-safe" is absent from every set on purpose.** It was the corporate buyer's own third filter. It is not a claim in the `CLAUDE.md` S7 safe-claims list, and Almond Delight declares tree nuts. The gap is deliberate and it costs this set something.
3. **"Batches ship from 10 October" is a dated promise in two places.** G5-A description 2 and G5-B headline 5. If the kitchen schedule moves, both move. A dispatch date in a gifting ad is the one line a buyer will hold the brand to.
4. **Landing pages for four of these sitelinks may not exist.** Corporate Enquiry, Branded Sleeves, Dispatch Dates and Wedding Favours are all `{placeholder}` until confirmed. `CLAUDE.md` S7 says the founder handles corporate accounts personally, which suggests the motion runs on WhatsApp rather than on a page. A sitelink pointing nowhere is a disapproval, not a click.
5. **"Talk to me on WhatsApp" is first person singular, which is the brand's rule,** and it puts the founder in the reply path for every corporate click. `CLAUDE.md` S7 says he already handles these accounts personally, and the Market Analyst report warns that if demand generation switches on, the founder becomes the bottleneck within days. That is an operations flag, not a copy flag, and it belongs to whoever answers the phone.
6. **No health claim in any set.** Nothing about digestion, gut, immunity or detox. Herbal Mukhwas, the highest-risk SKU in the file, does not appear in this angle at all.
7. **No competitor named, no comparison made,** even though every set here exists because of competitor activity. Every competitor price in the Market Analyst report is marked unverified.
8. **No bid or budget figure.** Corporate gifting keywords in a 59 day festival window will not price like this account's ₹245 branded CAC. Anything I wrote there would be invented. `{placeholder}`.
